Mathematics that moves under your hand.

Three interactive playgrounds make the observatory do mathematics, not just display it. Slide along the critical line and watch Riemann zeta cross zero; scrub the prime-counting function and see how far π(x) strays from Li(x); or trace the von Mangoldt staircase and watch the error collapse into the √X RH envelope. Every plot is computed live in your browser — no server, no API, no tracking.
