Three named results that anchor the Mathocalypse

🏆 Erdős unit distance conjecture — disproved

Combinatorial geometry · 2024–2025 — OpenAI disproved Erdős's 1946 conjecture using algebraic number theory constructions yielding more unit-distance pairs than the square grid.

🏆 Unique Games Conjecture — Lean-verified

Computational complexity · 2024–2025 — AI produced Lean-certified proofs incorporating novel error-correcting codes. Pins SDP as optimal approximation under P ≠ NP.

🏆 Unitary Synthesis Problem — refuted

Quantum computing · 2024–2025 — Aaronson-Kuperberg 2007 conjecture refuted by construction. Polynomial oracle queries suffice for arbitrary unitaries.

The narrative arc

Scott Aaronson joins Brian Greene to examine what happens to mathematics now that AI is solving problems that stumped humans for decades. The conversation names three breakthroughs and asks what humans are for when the machine is the most powerful theorem prover on Earth. The human role shifts from theorem-generator to archaeologist — using a math telescope to mine AI output. The Mathocalypse is not coming; it is here.
