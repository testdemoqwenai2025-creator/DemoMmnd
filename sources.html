Three named results that anchor the Mathocalypse

🏆

Erdős unit distance conjecture — disproved

Combinatorial geometry · 2024–2025

OpenAI's model disproved Erdős's 1946 conjecture that the maximum number of unit-distance pairs among n points in the plane is achieved by a regular square grid. The model synthesized obscure results from algebraic number theory to construct point configurations yielding strictly more unit-distance pairs than the grid.

Reference: OpenAI technical report (cited in source as [7, 8])

🏆

Unique Games Conjecture — Lean-verified proof

Computational complexity · 2024–2025

AI produced Lean-certified proofs for the UGC (Khot, 2001), incorporating novel error-correcting codes. If P ≠ NP, this pins SDP relaxation as the optimal polynomial-time approximation for a broad class of optimization problems.

Reference: cited in source as [12, 14]

🏆

Unitary Synthesis Problem — refuted

Quantum computing · 2024–2025

Aaronson and Kuperberg's 2007 conjecture that arbitrary n-qubit unitaries require Ω(2^n) oracle queries was disproved by construction. An oracle function exists allowing polynomial-time quantum state synthesis, with implications for black hole information complexity.

Reference: Aaronson's own conjecture, refuted; cited in source as [33–35]

The narrative arc

This World Science Festival conversation — part of the Rethinking Reality series supported by the John Templeton Foundation — brings Scott Aaronson (director of the Quantum Information Center at UT Austin, formerly two years on OpenAI's alignment team) into dialogue with Brian Greene. The subject is what Aaronson and others have begun calling the Mathocalypse (or Mathacre): the moment when agentic AI systems crossed from solving high-school and Olympiad mathematics to bulldozing career-defining open research problems.

Aaronson's opening position is uncompromising: anyone who still maintains that AI has not unseaten human mathematicians as the most powerful theorem prover on Earth is, in his word, delusional. Three recent breakthroughs anchor the claim. The benchmark is no longer "AI solves a problem quickly" but "AI solves problems that humans had collectively given up on within a working lifetime."

The mechanics of discovery are unusual: AI excels at parallelized, exhaustive search through thousands of unconventional constructions without fatigue or discouragement. The human role is transitioning from theorem-generator to archaeologist — using a math telescope to mine AI output for underlying insights. The Centaur model (human + AI team) becomes the canonical research dynamic.

Aaronson is emphatic that humans are needed more urgently now than at almost any point in the past. He argues for NTSB/FAA-style independent oversight, whistleblower protections, and international treaties against unmonitored recursive self-improvement. The conversation closes on consciousness: digital AIs generate output from probability distributions that can be resampled, backed up, or cloned infinitely. Human experience and art derive value from ephemerality, physical unclonability, and finite existence.

Twelve results transcribed across six languages

Each mathematical expression is transcribed into one of the observatory's six languages — rotated so all six are demonstrated, each appearing exactly twice.

1. Erdős unit distance conjecture (disproved)Python
Expression
$$u(n) = O\left(n^{1 + \frac{c}{\log \log n}}\right)$$
Intent

Erdős 1946 conjectured the maximum unit-distance pairs among n points is maximized by a square grid. AI disproved by algebraic-number-theoretic constructions.

2. Unique Games Conjecture (Lean-verified)TypeScript
Expression
$$\forall \varepsilon, \delta > 0, \exists k : \text{NP-hard to decide } \mathrm{SAT}_{\max} \geq 1-\varepsilon \text{ vs. } \mathrm{SAT}_{\max} \leq \delta$$
Intent

Khot 2001. Pins SDP relaxation as optimal approximation under P ≠ NP.

3. Unitary Synthesis Problem (refuted)Go
Expression
$$\exists f : \{0,1\}^* \to \{0,1\} : \forall U \in U(2^n), |C^f| = \mathrm{poly}(n)$$
Intent

Aaronson-Kuperberg 2007 conjecture refuted by construction. Implications for black hole complexity.

4. Schrödinger equationPython
Expression
$$i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial t} \Psi(\mathbf{r}, t) = \left[ -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2 + V(\mathbf{r}, t) \right] \Psi(\mathbf{r}, t)$$
Intent

The fundamental equation of non-relativistic quantum mechanics. Aaronson's quantum information work is grounded in the dynamics this equation governs.

5. Bekenstein-Hawking entropy (holographic principle)TypeScript
Expression
$$S_{\mathrm{BH}} = \frac{k_B A}{4 \ell_P^2}$$
Intent

Black hole entropy is proportional to horizon area, not volume. The holographic principle's foundational result.

6. Scaled dot-product attentionGo
Expression
$$\mathrm{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \mathrm{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) V$$
Intent

The core operation of modern transformer models — the same models bulldozing career-defining math problems.

7. Teleportation vs. path-findingScala
Expression
$$g^* = \mathrm{Model}_\theta(\mathrm{Prompt}), \quad s_0 \xrightarrow{\text{one-shot}} g^*$$
Intent

AI teleports from premise to theorem; human performs inverse path extraction to reconstruct understanding.

8. Information bottleneck objectiveScala
Expression
$$\min_{p(z|x)} \; I(X; Z) - \beta \, I(Z; Y)$$
Intent

Compress X into Z while preserving information about Y. Foundational to understanding what deep neural networks learn.

9. Gödel/Turing computability boundC
Expression
$$\forall F \text{ (sufficiently strong)} : \exists T : F \nvdash T \land F \nvdash \neg T$$
Intent

Absolute bound on automated theorem proving. Even unbounded AI cannot escape this.

10. Löb's theoremC
Expression
$$\vdash \Box(\Box P \to P) \;\implies\; \vdash \Box P$$
Intent

If a system proves that provability implies truth, then it proves everything. The self-trust trap in AI alignment.

11. Kolmogorov complexityElixir
Expression
$$K(x) = \min \{ |p| : U(p) = x \}$$
Intent

Formalizes AI's creativity as discovery of low-complexity bridges between distant concepts.

12. AIXI (universal AI)Elixir
Expression
$$a^* = \arg\max_a \sum_{\mu} 2^{-|\mu|} \sum_{o} V(o) \cdot p(o \mid a, \mu)$$
Intent

The theoretically optimal universal AI. Not implementable (requires solving the halting problem), but defines the upper bound on what any AI can achieve.
