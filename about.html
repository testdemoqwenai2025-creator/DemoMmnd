About

An observatory, not a website.

Mmnd treats a mathematical expression not as a decoration but as a seed. Each one enters the observatory alone — a single line of notation — and unfolds across five stages: into code, into observation, into deeper thought, into future possibility. The intent is to make the life of an idea visible, and to make that visibility durable.

The work is built around pairings across centuries. Leibniz and Turing, Euler and Shannon: not because they agreed, but because the infrastructure around their ideas changed so completely that the same notation means something different in each era.

Three principles

Open demo, closed IP

A public, no-NDA preview lives at DemoMmnd on GitHub Pages. The advanced source lives privately at Mmnd-Advanced. Auto-deployed by a single GitHub Action; no human in the loop after push.

Two-decade horizon

Every dependency is pinned, every asset is vendored locally, every reference is BibTeX. The site is a static export — no backend to rot, no service to sunset. It should still render in 2046.

Cross-era thought

Pairings span centuries. The intent is not comparison but conversation — the way an idea mutates across centuries, gaining and losing meaning as the surrounding infrastructure changes.
